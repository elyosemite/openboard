## Overview

The [[entity|Organization]] is the tenant: its data never crosses into another Organization (ADR 0003). A [[entity|Workspace]] divides one Organization and can restrict visibility inside it.

These rules are specific to OpenBoard, but they exist to support the core domain, Work Management. Teams and Projects belong here and have no code yet.
